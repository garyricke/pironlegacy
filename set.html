Straight answer on when these arrive

Card 3 is the only card that exists right now. It's the one we printed for the game. The other nine still have to be designed and printed, and a few of the photographs still need permission from the photographers who took them.

So: the set ships this fall, not next week. Give now and your name goes on the list — your tax acknowledgment letter comes on the normal schedule, and the cards follow when they're real. We'd rather tell you that than promise a date we can't hit.
