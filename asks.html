About 815 people: INCubator parents and alumni families, past Penrose sponsors, mentors and judges, and business partners. Smaller asks, $25 to $100, each one doubled. Students and school staff are left off.

From: Gary Ricke, Dennis Piron Legacy ScholarshipSubject: Coach Piron's last season, and a match that doubles your giftPreview: $25 becomes $50 for a Batavia senior.
Dennis Piron Legacy Scholarship

Hi Jordan,

For ten years Dennis Piron ran the INCubator at Batavia High School, and about 700 students learned to build a business in his classroom. You were part of that.

Dennis retired from teaching this year, and this fall is his final season as head football coach. A group of us started the Dennis Piron Legacy Scholarship so the next Batavia senior headed to college, a trade school or an apprenticeship gets the same backing his students got.

Every gift is doubled. The Dunham Foundation will match every dollar, up to $50,000, once our community half is raised. We're close.

Only the second sentence changes, so each group hears why it's them:

Parents and alumni familiesYour family was part of that.

One line to decide

"We're close" can become the real number: "We're close to unlocking the full $50,000 match." A specific gap moves people, but it means a public running total again. That was taken off the site in August, so it's the committee's call.

Send plan

  1. Now to Oct 26: committee makes the Tier A asks first, so lead gifts are in before the email goes out.
  2. Tue Oct 27: the email above, to all three groups.
  3. Tue Dec 1, Giving Tuesday: a short reminder to anyone who hasn't given.
  4. Tue Dec 29: last call before the tax year closes.

Before it goes out

  • Ashley removes anyone who has already given.
  • Dennis or Alex OKs using the INCubator list, and we keep clear of the spring Penrose fundraiser.
  • It goes out through a mailing tool with an unsubscribe link and BFEE's address, not a Gmail BCC.
