As Dennis Piron Retires From Teaching, Batavia Moves to Endow a Scholarship in His Name

BATAVIA, Ill. — As Dennis Piron retires from teaching at Batavia High School, a group of Batavia business leaders and former mentors has launched a campaign to permanently endow a scholarship in his name — honoring the decade he spent turning students into entrepreneurs through the school's INCubator program.

By the program's own estimate, nearly 700 students came through the class in those ten years.

The Dennis Piron Legacy Scholarship will be awarded every year to a graduating Batavia High School senior. Because the fund is endowed rather than raised year to year, the award continues indefinitely.

The campaign has a $100,000 goal. The Dunham Foundation has approved a $50,000 dollar-for-dollar matching grant, released once the community raises and delivers the first $50,000. More than $36,000 has already been committed. The scholarship is held by the Batavia Foundation for Educational Excellence, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit.

Piron is not leaving Batavia. He remains head football coach through the coming season, and stays with INCubator as a community champion alongside Bob Hubbard. The program's new teacher is Alexandra Piron — a Batavia High graduate who joined the school's math department last year and was selected from a field of candidates for the role. She is also his daughter.
