How to post

  • Instagram: post as a Reel (not a feed post) and share it to your Story too. Where a caption credits @cedjayphotos, tag him on the video as well.

  • YouTube: upload as a Short and use the title given with each video.
  • Facebook: post as a Reel. LinkedIn: upload the video file directly instead of sharing a link.
  • Every video ends on pironlegacy.org, and every caption spells it out, since nobody has the link in their bio.
