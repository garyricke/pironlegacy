Criteria meeting notes

Wednesday, November 18, 2026. What we decided about who receives the scholarship and what the application asks. If anything here doesn’t match what you remember, or you have a change, please email Gary.

Sources: Ashley Murtaugh’s email of November 18 and Gary’s recorded transcript of the meeting.

1 What the scholarship is for

To be awarded to a student for a trade school, educational program expenses, community college, or 4-year college/university who demonstrates drive and passion to pursue their future plans.

2 Criteria we decided

  • Who can apply: graduating seniors who have taken part in INCubator or Field Study (now or in an earlier year). Not open to every senior in this first year; we may widen it later.
  • Award: no more than $4,000 in total each year. The committee can give it to one student or split it among several, depending on who applies and what they need (for example, $750 for union dues).
  • How it’s paid: only to the school or educational program (a college, trade school, union or training program), never to the student. Money that isn’t needed comes back to the Foundation.
  • Format: a written essay (at least 300 words), a video (3 to 5 minutes), or both. Student’s choice.
  • Not required: GPA, transcripts or financial information.
  • Selection: a selection committee, with Tyler Piron taking part, chooses in the spring (around March).

3 Questions applicants answer

  1. How would this scholarship support your goals for the future?
  2. How has participating in INCubator or Field Study helped shape your goals for the future?
  3. In what ways have you positively impacted a team, at school, or the Batavia community, or plan to in the future?

4 Information the application collects

  • Name
  • Address
  • Phone
  • Personal email (not a BPS school address)
  • School or program they plan to attend
  • Major or industry
  • What the scholarship would pay for

The application doesn’t ask for payment details. Ashley at the Batavia Foundation sorts out the check with each student once they’re chosen.

5 Still to settle

  • Application open date and deadline (the goal is release in the New Year).
  • Who else sits on the selection committee.
