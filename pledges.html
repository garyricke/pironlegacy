Settings — committee emails & baseline

Committee members' own pledges are already inside the baseline, so they are not re-added to the counter. Paste their pledge-form emails here (one per line) so the ledger can flag them. A pledge from a different address on the same email domain is marked Review so you can decide.

Baseline = committee pledges already counted. “Site counter now” auto-fills from the live COMMITTED value when reachable.
