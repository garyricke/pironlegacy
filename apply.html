Dennis Piron Legacy Scholarship application

For graduating seniors who took part in INCubator or Field Study and are heading to a trade school, an educational program, community college, or a 4-year college or university.

1 About the scholarship

The scholarship goes to a student who demonstrates drive and passion to pursue their future plans.

  • Award: up to $4,000 this year, given to one student or shared among several
  • Paid to: your college, trade school, union or training program, not to you directly
  • Who can apply: graduating seniors who have taken part in INCubator or Field Study, now or in an earlier year
  • Your answers: three questions, answered either in writing (at least 300 words for each question) or in one 3 to 5 minute video that covers all three
  • No GPA, transcript or financial information needed. We want your story.
